# Integration research catalog — the 42 starred repositories (+ LangGraph)

**Status:** research record, not a dependency list and not approval. Linked from `../engineering/BUILD-AND-INTEGRATION-PLAN.md` §4. Dispositions are the founder's provisional dispositions of 2026-10-22, with the independent review of 2026-10-22 as the evidence basis. Facts marked **V** were checked against the project's GitHub page on 2026-10-22 by the independent reviewer; **U** means not fetched (low stakes). Star counts are deliberately omitted: popularity is not architectural evidence. Re-verify anything before immediate adoption. Dispositions: **Reference** (read only), **Pattern** (borrow an idea, no code), **Adapt** (copy or trial a bounded piece under its license), **Isolate** (run out of process behind a Sovereign contract, later), **Dependency** (ordinary library when justified), **Avoid** (do not use as a model or dependency).

**Mandatory strategic conclusions:** build the authority core, integrate capabilities behind contracts, keep model choice independent, isolate every runtime from the vault, treat skills and plugins as supply chain, prefer velocity gains that are text (methods) over gains that are hooks, and keep every adoption replaceable. Do not install all 42. Do not mix hypotheses with approved dependencies (see `../architecture/systems-registry.toml` for what is actually operational).
